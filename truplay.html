THE CREATIVE PREMISE / 01

Good creative gets attention.
Clear creative earns understanding.

For an App Store visitor, the first moments matter. The design needed to communicate what TruPlay offers, make content variety easy to recognize and carry one cohesive visual direction across the listing.

HOW I APPROACHED IT / 02

Design decisions, not decoration.

My contribution centered on visual design and iteration, with careful attention to the brief, supplied assets and stakeholder feedback.
