CASE STUDY / TRUPLAY / APP STORE CREATIVE

Designing for
clarity and discovery.

Selected creative contributions to TruPlay's App Store listing presentation, prepared in collaboration with Admiral Media. This is an independent portfolio explanation of my design role, not a reproduction of the agency's case-study website.

01 / CONTEXT

The creative challenge

Help a new visitor understand the variety of faith-based games, comics, and videos at a glance. The screens needed a clear first impression, legible messaging, and visual consistency across the landscape set.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

From design exploration to refinement

Visual hierarchy

Clarifying the first-screen value proposition and making the offering understandable at a glance.

Creative production

Creating and compositing imagery for the screenshot series while maintaining alignment with source characters and artwork.

Iteration

Updating screen captions and aligning imagery with stakeholder feedback and 4+ content suitability.

Original materials and attribution
